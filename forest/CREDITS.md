# SPOOKY FOREST credits

These packs are CC0 1.0 (public domain). Credit is not required. It is included here and on the title screen anyway.

- Hero body, long hair, and the idle, walk, run, crouch, jump, and swing clips: Quaternius, Universal Base Characters and Universal Animation Library. https://quaternius.com
- Monster body and its clips: Quaternius, Cute Animated Monsters (Alien Tall), recolored red. https://quaternius.com
- Trees, rocks, grass, bushes, logs, and stumps: Quaternius, Ultimate Nature Pack. https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-nature-pack-1
- Ground, bark, and rock textures: ambientCG (Ground037, Bark012, Rock035). https://ambientcg.com

The tiara, cape, bracers, boots, skirt, lasso rope, and the monster's eyes are simple attachments added on top of those packs.

The lasso swing uses the upper body of Quaternius's Sword_Attack clip from the Universal Animation Library, blended over the walk and run so she can keep moving. The rope itself is drawn in the game.

Poly Haven's Lantern_01 is CC0, but the mesh buffer alone is about 865 KB before textures. The lantern in the forest is an original low-poly iron cage with glass panes, meshopt-compressed, with a flame and glow added in the game.
